# 🤖 AI Smart Study Planner

An **AI-powered Smart Study Planner** that helps students organize their study schedules based on subjects, priorities, available study time, deadlines, and learning goals.

The system creates a personalized study plan and helps students manage their academic workload efficiently.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Students often struggle to manage multiple subjects, assignments, exams, and deadlines at the same time. A fixed timetable may not work for every student because study time and subject difficulty can vary.

The **AI Smart Study Planner** provides a personalized planning approach by considering the student's:

* 📚 Subjects
* ⏰ Available study hours
* 🎯 Study goals
* 🔥 Subject priority
* 📅 Exam and assignment deadlines
* 📊 Progress and completion status

Based on these factors, the system generates a structured and manageable study schedule.

## 🧠 Smart Scheduling Logic

The planner considers different factors while generating the study schedule.

### Priority

```text
High Priority → More study time
Medium Priority → Moderate study time
Low Priority → Less study time
```

### Deadline

Tasks with approaching deadlines receive higher priority.

### Available Time

The system distributes tasks according to the student's available study hours.

### Progress

Completed tasks are removed or reduced from future schedules, allowing the planner to adapt.
